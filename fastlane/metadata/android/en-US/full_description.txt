Point your camera at anything around you and a monster that looks just like it is born. Collect them, battle with them, and show them off.

YOUR PHOTO BECOMES A MONSTER
Take a photo or pick one from your library. The area you pick on the photo becomes the monster as it is. Its name, dex entry, elements, moves, and stats come from what the subject is. Once a monster is born, its look and abilities never change.

TURN-BASED BATTLES WHERE STATS MATTER
Monsters act in order of speed. Move power, elemental weaknesses, attack and defense, and remaining PP all decide the outcome. Take your party of three into battle against CPU trainers, and level up after every battle.

FILL YOUR DEX
Every monster you create is kept in your dex.

SHARE
Turn dex cards and battle results into images and share them on social media. A monster's art can be part or all of the original photo, and anyone who sees a shared card can see it.

PLAY SAFELY
• Monster names and dex entries are generated by AI
• Monsters are not generated from inappropriate photos
• You get a notice when a monster is made from a photo with a person in it
• Get their permission first if someone else is in the photo
• Link your Google account to keep your dex when you change devices
• For ages 13 and up
