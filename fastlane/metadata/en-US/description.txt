Point your camera at anything around you and a monster that looks just like it is born. Collect them, battle with them, and show them off.

YOUR PHOTO BECOMES A MONSTER
Take a photo or pick one from your library. The subject is cut out of the photo and dressed up with eyes, limbs, and an elemental aura. Its name, dex entry, elements, moves, and stats come from what the subject is. Once a monster is born, its look and abilities never change.

TURN-BASED BATTLES WHERE STATS MATTER
Monsters act in order of speed. Move power, elemental weaknesses, attack and defense, and remaining PP all decide the outcome. Take your party of three into battle against CPU trainers and other players' parties, and level up after every battle.

FILL YOUR DEX
Every monster is kept in your dex next to its original photo. Only you can see your original photos. Other players never see them.

SHARE
Turn dex cards and battle results into images and share them on social media. Shared cards never include the original photo.

PLAY SAFELY
• Monster names and dex entries are generated by AI
• Monsters are not generated from photos with faces or inappropriate content
• You can report inappropriate monsters and block players you meet in battles
• Link your Apple ID to keep your dex when you change devices
• For ages 13 and up
